Privacy Policy

WelSimulation LLC (“WelSim,” “us,” “we,” or “our”) protects any information that can be used to identify you directly or indirectly (“Personal Data”). This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) describes the kind of information WelSim may collect or otherwise process during your visit to or use of:

  • the www.welsim.com website, docs.welsim.com, and any other WelSim websites on which this Policy appears (the “WelSim Ecosystem”);
  • the WelSim software products and services made available through the WelSim Ecosystem, including their networked features such as license activation and verification, update checks, and the optional crash-report feature; and
  • community, support, sales, beta-testing, and account-management interactions with WelSim.

By accessing, viewing, browsing, and otherwise using the WelSim Ecosystem or the WelSim products, you agree with this Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, you should not access, view, browse, or otherwise use them.

As of November 10, 2026

1. What Information Do We Collect, and How?

1.1 Information you provide to us. You may share information about you by corresponding with us, or filling in forms in the WelSim Ecosystem, including information you provide when requesting a quote or purchase, activating a license, reporting a problem with the WelSim Ecosystem or the WelSim products, participating in the WelSim beta testing program, contacting support, or by corresponding with us by phone, email or otherwise. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, mailing and billing address, company or organization affiliation, payment-related information, and the contents of your communications.

1.2 Information we collect automatically. As is true of most websites, WelSim gathers certain information automatically and stores it in log files when you visit the WelSim Ecosystem. This includes aggregated data such as internet protocol (IP) address, operating system, browser type and language, internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, date/time stamps, session activity, clickstream data, approximate location derived from IP address, and your interactions with service features and content. We use this information, which does not identify individual users, to analyze trends, to administer the WelSim Ecosystem, to track users’ movements around the WelSim Ecosystem, and to gather demographic information about our user base. We do not link this automatically collected data to personally identifiable information. We may disclose aggregated information that does not identify any individual or device without restriction.

1.3 Software usage and diagnostic information. The WelSim products include networked features: license activation and verification and update checks contact WelSim or release servers with the technical identifiers needed to perform those functions. If the WelSim products stop unexpectedly, a crash-report dialog may appear describing the files that would be sent (a crash dump, its diagnostic sidecar, and your preferences file); this report and your email address are transmitted to WelSim only when you explicitly choose to send them, and they are used solely to diagnose and fix the problem. You can decline, and the crash files remain only on your own computer.

1.4 Tracking technologies. The WelSim Ecosystem uses cookies. Cookies are small data files stored on your hard drive by a website. Cookies help WelSim to make the WelSim Ecosystem and your visit better. We may also use cookies to see which parts of the WelSim Ecosystem people use and like, and to count visits. We process the cookie data collected in accordance with this Policy. You may, at any time, permanently deny the setting of cookies by changing the setting of your Internet browser. This is possible in all popular Internet browsers. However, deactivating the setting of cookies may change the availability of some functions available through the WelSim Ecosystem. You can learn more about how cookies work at: https://allaboutcookies.org/.

1.5 Information from third parties. We may also collect information from third-party services such as payment processors, authentication providers, analytics providers, and infrastructure providers in connection with the WelSim Ecosystem and the WelSim products.

2. What Legal Basis Do We Have to Collect Your Information?

WelSim collects Personal Data only when we have a legal basis to do so. In most cases, and where processing of Personal Data does not unduly infringe your rights and freedoms, WelSim relies on our legitimate interests in conducting normal business operations. Our legitimate interests include, but are not limited to, enhancing the security of our network and information systems, carrying out processing operations for statistical purposes, and identifying or preventing fraud. We may need to process your Personal Data in order to negotiate or perform on a contract to which you are a party. In some cases, we may have to process your Personal Data to comply with laws and regulations. Where processing rests on your consent — for example when you choose to send a crash report — you may withdraw that consent at any time (see Section 7).

3. How Do We Use Your Information?

We use the information obtained through the WelSim Ecosystem and the WelSim products to provide, operate, maintain, support, and improve them; to process quotes, purchases, licenses, and payments; to authenticate users and administer accounts; to communicate regarding accounts, updates, security notices, and customer support; to monitor and improve performance and reliability, including diagnosing crashes you choose to report; for internal business analysis; and to prevent fraud, violations of our Terms of Use, and illegal activities, and to protect our rights and the rights and safety of our users or others.

4. Does WelSim Share My Personal Data?

WelSim is not in the business of selling your Personal Data. However, your Personal Data may be shared with others in accordance with this Policy in the following circumstances:

4.1 Third-party service providers. Third-party providers employed by WelSim to perform functions on our behalf may require access to Personal Data to perform their services. Such functions may include data analysis, payment processing, content transmission, hosting, and customer service. All third-party providers must process Personal Data in accordance with this Policy and as permitted by applicable data protection laws.

4.2 Protection of WelSim and others. WelSim may release Personal Data to comply with the law or with regulatory obligations; to enforce or apply our Terms of Use and other agreements; to protect WelSim’s rights, property, and safety; and to protect the rights, property, and safety of others.

4.3 Corporate transactions. We may transfer or disclose Personal Data to a third party in the event of an actual or proposed sale, merger, reorganization, or other restructuring of WelSimulation LLC.

5. How Secure Is My Personal Data?

The WelSim Ecosystem is designed with security in mind. We use encryption protocols and software to protect your Personal Data during transmission, and work to maintain administrative, physical, and procedural safeguards when collecting, storing, or disclosing your Personal Data. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure; if we become aware that the security of your Personal Data has been compromised, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

6. How Long Does WelSim Keep My Personal Data?

We keep your Personal Data for as long as needed to enable your continued use of the WelSim Ecosystem and the WelSim products, for as long as required to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, as may be required by law such as for tax and accounting purposes, or as otherwise communicated to you. Crash reports you send are kept only as long as needed to investigate the reported problem.

7. What Are My Privacy Rights and Choices?

If you consented to WelSim’s processing of your Personal Data for a specified purpose, you may withdraw your consent at any time. Subject to applicable law, you have the right to request access to, correct, and delete your Personal Data, and to ask for data portability. You may also object to our processing of your Personal Data or ask that we restrict the processing of your Personal Data in certain instances. If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact info@welsim.com. In some cases we may not be able to act on a request — for example when doing so would conflict with applicable law — and we will explain why. You can also exercise choices through your tools: your browser can refuse or delete cookies (Section 1.4), and crash reports are sent only if you submit them (Section 1.3).

8. Personal Data of Minors

The WelSim Ecosystem and the WelSim products are not directed to children under thirteen (13) years of age, or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction if higher, and we do not knowingly collect Personal Data from such children. If you are between 13 and 18, you may use the WelSim Ecosystem only with the permission and under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian. If we learn that we have collected Personal Data from a child below the applicable age, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information or deactivate the account, as appropriate.

9. Other Websites and External Links

The WelSim Ecosystem may contain links to other websites or social-media buttons. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of those websites or platforms; this Policy applies only to the WelSim Ecosystem and the WelSim products.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Policy, we post those changes on the WelSim Ecosystem and update the date of the Policy. If the changes are material, we will also provide a notice on our website.
